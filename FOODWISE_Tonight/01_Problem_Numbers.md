# FOODWISE — Problem numbers

## Headline figures

| Metric | Value | Source / context |
|---|---:|---|
| People who cannot afford a healthy diet | **43.5–43.9 million** (approximately **76–77%**) | FAO 2024/2025 figures supplied in the project brief |
| Daily cost of a healthy diet | **KSh 189–KSh 582** (varies by method) | FAO / Cost of a Healthy Diet figures supplied in the project brief |
| Food inflation, September 2026 | **9.5%** | KNBS: annual inflation for Food and Non-Alcoholic Beverages |
| Key rising items to monitor | **Milk, wheat flour, cabbage, potatoes** | Items supplied in the project brief as KNBS CPI priorities |

## How to present these numbers responsibly

- The KNBS figure is **year-on-year inflation for the Food and Non-Alcoholic Beverages division** in September 2026. It is not the overall CPI inflation rate. KNBS's release page reports overall annual CPI inflation of 6.8%.
- The 43.5–43.9 million / 76–77% range and KSh 189–582 daily cost are briefing figures. Before external publication, attach the exact FAO release/table, edition, reference year, geographic definition and method. Do not present the KSh range as a single current retail price.
- Treat the listed milk, wheat flour, cabbage and potato items as the project's supplied priority list until the corresponding KNBS item-level table has been checked and cited.
- WFP market prices are useful for local signals, but they are not a nationally representative CPI or a complete household food basket.
