# FOODWISE — Problem numbers and claims

## Reference figures for the showcase

| Metric | Value | Source / qualification |
|---|---:|---|
| People who cannot afford a healthy diet | **43.5–43.9 million** (approximately **76–77%**) | Supplied in the project brief as FAO 2024/2025 figures. Verify the exact CoAHD series, reference year and denominator before public use. |
| Daily cost of a healthy diet | **KSh 189–KSh 582** (method-dependent) | Supplied in the project brief as FAO / Cost of a Healthy Diet estimates. Do not describe as one current WFP market price. |
| Food inflation, September 2026 | **9.5%** | KNBS release: annual change for the Food and Non-Alcoholic Beverages CPI division. |
| Overall CPI inflation, September 2026 | **6.8%** | KNBS release; distinguishes the food-divisions figure from overall CPI. |
| Food items flagged in the brief | Milk, wheat flour, cabbage, potatoes | Priorities supplied by the brief; confirm item-level movements directly in the KNBS report before saying they were the largest increases. |

## Safe wording

“KNBS reported 9.5% year-on-year inflation in Food and Non-Alcoholic Beverages in September 2026. WFP's Kenya dataset provides reported market prices, but its market coverage is uneven. FOODWISE combines neither series into an official inflation measure.”
